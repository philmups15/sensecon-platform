13 — Imagery & motifs

Sencecon's visual language stays flat, geometric, and specific to solar infrastructure — never generic tech stock.

Do

Flat geometric icons, real site/plant photography, tabular data shown plainly.

Don't

Glossy 3D globes, neon gradient "AI" glows, generic hardhat-handshake stock photos.
